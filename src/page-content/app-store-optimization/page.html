The Data Sources and Optimization Systems Behind Every ASO Engagement

ASO does not stop at the store page. The factors that influence an app’s ranking and conversion reach into adjacent layers of the product and its marketing infrastructure.

Apple’s native analytics surfaces impression volume, page view rates, conversion funnels, and keyword performance directly. The team reads this data regularly to track what is working and where the page is losing installs.

Google Play Console

Google’s native dashboard exposes store listing performance, acquisition breakdown, and conversion data by traffic source, mirroring the platform-specific depth behind Android app development decisions made further upstream. Both platforms offer native A/B testing for creative assets, the same experiments that matter most once you publish or update an app on the App Store and Google Play. Structured experiments with defined variables and measured outcomes replace gut-check creative decisions, with testing cadence built into every engagement from day one.

A/B Testing and Focus Groups

Creative and metadata variations are tested against real user segments before going live. Focus groups add a qualitative layer to quantitative test data, so decisions reflect both behavioral patterns and genuine user perception.

FAQs

App Store Optimization Services

What is App Store Optimization and why does it matter?

App Store Optimization (ASO) is the practice of improving an app’s visibility and conversion rate within app store search results through keyword, metadata, and creative optimization. It matters because most installs originate from organic search, and a poorly optimized listing keeps even strong apps buried beneath competitors.

How long does it take to see results from ASO?

Keyword rank changes typically show within four to eight weeks. Conversion improvements from creative testing often appear sooner, within two to three weeks of deployment.

Does ASO work differently for iOS and Android?

Yes, significantly. Both stores use different ranking algorithms, metadata fields, and creative specs. Each platform gets its own strategy rather than a single approach applied to both.

What is the difference between ASO and paid app marketing?

Paid acquisition buys installs at a cost per click. ASO builds organic visibility that compounds over time. Both work better together, but ASO does not stop when the budget does.

How do you decide which keywords to target?

The team maps search volume, competition, and relevance across your category. Keywords are chosen based on where the app can realistically rank and what users searching those terms actually intend to do.

Do you handle creative production as part of ASO?

Yes. Screenshot design, preview video production, and icon refinement are included in Growth and Scale engagements. Every asset gets tested through the store’s native A/B environments before being kept.

Can ASO help an app that has never been optimized before?

That is the most common starting point. The audit at the beginning of the engagement identifies the gaps quickly, and first-time optimization often produces the most significant early gains.

How do you approach ASO for apps in competitive categories?

The team focuses on keyword opportunities where the app can rank realistically alongside conversion improvements that hold regardless of competitive intensity. Both levers work simultaneously.

What does your reporting cover?

Every reporting cycle covers keyword rank movement, conversion rates, impression volume, install trends, and review metrics. Each number ties back to a defined benchmark, so context is never missing.

Do you work with apps at early stages or only with established products?

Both. Early-stage products benefit from getting the store presence right before launch, often as part of a wider startup product development engagement. Established apps often have metadata and creative issues that an audit surfaces and resolves quickly.

How many platforms do you optimize for?

The studio optimizes for both the App Store and Google Play. Each platform is treated separately because what drives rank and conversion on one does not automatically transfer to the other.
