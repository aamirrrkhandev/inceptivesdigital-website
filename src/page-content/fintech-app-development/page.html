Questions About Fintech App Development Worth Asking Before You Build

How long does fintech app development take?

A basic fintech application takes 3 to 6 months. Complex platforms with advanced AI, blockchain integration, and multi-bank connectivity take 9 months or more. The timeline is defined precisely in the Discovery Sprint based on scope, compliance requirements, and integration complexity.

How do you handle financial data security?

End-to-end encryption, secure APIs, biometric authentication, and strict access controls are applied across every fintech application. PCI DSS, GDPR, and KYC/AML compliance are built into the architecture from day one. Not added before delivery.

Do you offer post-launch maintenance?

Yes. Regular updates, feature enhancements, security patches, and performance monitoring are provided continuously after deployment. Financial applications require ongoing compliance alignment as regulations evolve — that is part of the post-launch engagement.

Can you integrate with banks and payment systems?

Yes. Financial APIs, payment gateways, and banking networks are integrated securely into every fintech application. Plaid, Stripe, PayPal, and major banking network connectivity are built for reliable, real-time transactions.

What compliance standards do you follow?

PCI DSS, GDPR, PSD2, KYC, and AML compliance are built into every fintech application. Every data flow, authentication layer, and transaction pathway is designed around regulatory requirements before development begins.
