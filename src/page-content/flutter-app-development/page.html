How Much Does Flutter App Development Cost?

Flutter app development cost runs from $15,000 for simple apps to $150,000 and above for enterprise-grade products with complex backend and integration requirements, and the same factors affecting app development cost apply whether the build is Flutter, native, or something hybrid.

Simple App

$15k - $40k

A focused MVP or single-purpose app with core screens, light integrations, and a straightforward user flow, priced closer to the lower end of typical MVP development cost figures. Built for teams that need to validate an idea quickly without overbuilding from the start.

Moderate App

$40k - $80k

Multi-flow products with richer UI, standard third-party integrations, authentication, and consistent performance across iOS and Android. Suited for businesses moving beyond MVP into a production-ready product.

Complex App

$80k - $150k+

Highly complex products with custom backend architecture, real-time features, advanced security, AI or IoT integrations, and ongoing optimization requirements. Built for products that need to perform at scale from launch.

Flutter App Development Services

What is Flutter, and why is it used for app development?

Flutter is Google’s open-source framework that builds natively compiled apps for mobile, web, and desktop from a single codebase, cutting development time significantly.

What platforms does Flutter support?

Flutter supports iOS, Android, web, Windows, macOS, and Linux from a single codebase. Our iOS app development work runs alongside every other platform your product needs, without maintaining separate builds.

How much does Flutter app development cost?

Most Flutter products we build range from $15,000 to $150,000, depending on complexity, integrations, and design requirements, a spread that lines up with general mobile app development cost benchmarks across the industry. Every estimate is scoped after a discovery session.

Is Flutter good for large-scale enterprise products?

Absolutely. Google, Nubank, and BMW each run mission-critical products on Flutter, including regulated categories like fintech app development, where reliability at scale isn’t optional. Our team has delivered enterprise-scale Flutter products across multiple industries with the same rigor.

What do your Flutter web app development services include?

Our Flutter web app development services cover the full build, from UI and backend integration to deployment, performance optimization, and post-launch support across all browsers.

Can an existing native app be migrated to Flutter?

Yes. We handle the full migration process, including codebase consolidation, feature parity validation, and performance benchmarking.

Does Flutter perform as well as native apps?

Flutter uses its own rendering engine rather than platform UI libraries. The products we build perform at native quality across every device without platform-specific compromises.

Can Flutter apps integrate with AI, IoT, and blockchain?

Yes. We integrate AI, IoT, blockchain development, AR/VR, and 5G capabilities into Flutter products where the use case genuinely calls for them, not as additions for their own sake.

What happens after the app is launched?

We handle ongoing maintenance covering bug fixes, platform updates, performance monitoring, and new feature development as part of a continuous support engagement after launch.
