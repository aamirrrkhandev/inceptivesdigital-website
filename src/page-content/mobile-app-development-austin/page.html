Frequently Asked Questions About Mobile App Development in Austin

How much does mobile app development cost in Austin?

Custom mobile app development in Austin starts at $25,000 for an MVP. Complex platforms with advanced integrations run $80,000 to $150,000 and above. The precise number is defined in the Discovery Sprint based on your compliance requirements, platform targets, and integration complexity.

How long does it take to build a mobile app in Austin?

An MVP takes 8 to 12 weeks from kickoff to App Store submission. Larger platforms with custom backend systems run for 3 to 6 months. The timeline is confirmed in Discovery before development begins.

Should I build a native or cross-platform app?

Native apps, Swift for iOS and Kotlin for Android, deliver the highest performance and full device hardware access. Cross-platform development through Flutter or React Native runs on both platforms from one codebase, cutting build time significantly. The decision comes down to performance demands and how fast you need to ship — confirmed in Discovery.

Why should I hire a local mobile app development company in Austin?

A local team understands TDPSA compliance, the Austin market pace, and the specific expectations of SaaS, healthtech, fintech, and ClimateTech sectors. Local collaboration also makes communication, project management, and long-term support more efficient.

What questions should I ask before hiring a mobile app developer?

Ask about their architecture approach, how they handle scope changes, who your daily contact will be, and what post-launch support looks like. The right team has straight answers to all of it before you ask.

Can I launch an MVP before investing in a full platform?

Yes. The Foundation tier starts at $25,000 for a focused MVP. A discovery sprint defines the precise scope and cost before development begins, so you know exactly what you are committing to.

What does post-launch support include?

Every project includes a 30-day post-launch support window as standard. Ongoing maintenance packages cover performance monitoring, crash reporting, security patches, OS compatibility updates, and App Store Optimization.

Do you sign NDAs before discussing my app idea?

Yes. A mutual NDA is signed before any discovery conversation starts. Every concept, architecture decision, and business detail shared stays fully confidential throughout the engagement.
