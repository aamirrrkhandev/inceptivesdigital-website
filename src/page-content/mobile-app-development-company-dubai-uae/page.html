iOS App Development

Swift and SwiftUI remain the foundation for iPhone and iPad applications where performance influences product success. Every release is engineered to align with Apple’s evolving ecosystem, App Store requirements, and the expectations of modern users.

Android App Development

Android products require careful engineering across diverse devices, operating system versions, and hardware configurations. Kotlin-based development focuses on scalable architecture and consistent user experiences that remain stable as the Android ecosystem continues to expand.

Cross-Platform App Development

The right platform strategy depends on product goals, release timelines, and future maintenance requirements. Cross-platform development creates a shared foundation for iOS and Android applications while helping teams improve delivery efficiency without sacrificing scalability or product quality.

React Native Development

When product timelines demand faster iteration across platforms, React Native provides a practical balance between delivery speed and application quality. A shared codebase reduces unnecessary engineering duplication while maintaining the performance required for continuous product advancement.

Flutter App Development

Maintaining a consistent experience across platforms requires more than replicating screens. Flutter enables teams to build unified interfaces with smooth performance, allowing products to preserve visual quality, accelerate iteration, and evolve efficiently across iOS and Android.

Kotlin Multiplatform Development

Some products require shared engineering efficiency while preserving fully native experiences. Kotlin Multiplatform allows teams to share core business logic across platforms while maintaining platform-specific interfaces, creating a flexible architecture built for long-term evolution.

Backend and API Development

Web Application Development

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does mobile app development cost in Dubai?
Mobile app development cost in Dubai typically ranges from AED 110,000 for an MVP to AED 550,000+ for enterprise platforms. The final investment depends on product scope, technical complexity, and integrations, with a fixed proposal prepared following the discovery phase.
How long does it take to build a mobile app in Dubai?
Most MVPs take three to six months from discovery through App Store and Google Play submission. Builds with custom infrastructure, AI features, or UAE Pass integration typically extend that timeline, depending on how many external systems the product needs to integrate with.
What is the difference between native and cross-platform app development?
Native development builds separately for iOS and Android using Swift and Kotlin, best suited to performance-heavy features. Cross-platform development, through Flutter or React Native, shares one codebase across both platforms, cutting timeline and long-term maintenance cost.
What data protection laws apply to mobile apps built in Dubai?
UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45, the PDPL, governs data residency, consent management, and breach notification for apps operating in Dubai. Inceptives Digital builds these requirements into the architecture from the discovery phase rather than treating them as a post-launch compliance check.
Do mobile apps built for the Dubai market need multilingual support?
Most do. Dubai’s resident and customer base regularly switches between Arabic, English, Hindi, and Urdu, so interfaces typically need Arabic right-to-left layouts alongside these languages from the first wireframe rather than as a later localization pass.
