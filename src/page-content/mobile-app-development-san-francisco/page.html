Frequently Asked Questions

How Much Does It Cost to Build a Mobile App in San Francisco?
Most mobile apps fall into three ranges: Foundation apps ($25k-$60k), Advanced platforms ($60k-$150k), and Enterprise systems ($150k+). Cost is primarily driven by complexity, integrations, compliance requirements, and the number of user roles involved.
How Long Does It Take to Develop a Mobile App?
Most projects take between 8 and 32 weeks from discovery to launch. Simpler products ship faster, while larger platforms with integrations and multiple user types naturally require more time.
Can I Build an App Without Technical Experience?
Yes. Many founders we work with are industry experts rather than technical founders. A good product partner translates business requirements into product and technical decisions.
How Do I Know If My App Idea Is Worth Building?
Validation should happen before development begins. The goal is to understand whether the problem is painful enough and frequent enough for people to adopt a solution.
Should I Build for iOS or Android First?
For most startups, launching on both platforms simultaneously is the better decision. Cross-platform technologies make this possible without doubling development costs.
What's the Difference Between Native and Cross-Platform Apps?
Native apps are built separately for iOS and Android, while cross-platform apps share a single codebase across both. The right choice depends on performance requirements, budget, and long-term product goals.
What's Included in Mobile App Development Services?
Most engagements include product discovery, UX design, development, backend infrastructure, testing, deployment, and post-launch support. Building the product is only one part of launching it successfully.
What Makes a Good Mobile App Development Partner?
The best partners think beyond development. They should be able to discuss product strategy, scalability, compliance, and business outcomes, not just technology choices.
