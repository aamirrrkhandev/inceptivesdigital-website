The Business Challenge

  • Family Entertainment Group operates attraction-based venues where the experience itself is the product. Roller coasters, escape rooms, and interactive attractions create moments guests want to remember and share.
  • The problem was not creating memorable experiences.
  • It was capturing them.
  • Guests increasingly expected professional-quality video content of their visit, but producing that content manually created an operational bottleneck that did not scale with venue traffic.
  • Staff was responsible for:
  • Managing camera recordings
  • Transferring footage manually
  • Editing videos
  • Delivering finished files to guests
  • The workflow was slow, inconsistent, and dependent on staff having skills they were never hired for.
  • Every guest who left without receiving a video represented:
  • Lost ancillary revenue
  • Lost social exposure
  • Lost marketing reach
  • Lost post-visit engagement
  • The venue already had GoPro cameras installed throughout its attractions.
  • What it lacked was the product infrastructure connecting those cameras to a guest-ready delivery experience.

What We Built

  • Rather than building another media management tool, we designed an automated production pipeline that transformed raw footage into branded highlight videos without human intervention.
  • The platform consisted of two tightly connected products:

Staff Capture Application

  • Built with Flutter for tablets and mobile devices.
  • Used by venue staff to:
  • Connect and monitor GoPro cameras
  • Register guest sessions
  • Preview live footage
  • Trigger automated processing
  • Deliver videos through email or SMS
  • The experience was intentionally simplified for high-volume environments where speed mattered more than flexibility.
  • No editing skills required.
  • No training dependency.
  • No production decisions

Core
Product Decisions

Three architectural decisions shaped the platform.

Automated Editing as Core Infrastructure

The editing engine became the product itself rather than a supporting feature.

The platform automatically:

Combined footage from multiple cameras
Applied branded intros and outros
Added music overlays
Rendered finished videos
Prepared delivery assets
No manual intervention existed between recording and delivery.

Dual-Camera Synchronization

Each experience used synchronized multi-angle recording through existing GoPro infrastructure.
This created:
More cinematic output
Better replay value
Higher perceived quality
Greater social shareability
The result felt closer to professional production than to consumer video capture.

Instant Delivery Pipeline

Delivery speed directly influenced sharing behavior.
Videos were automatically delivered via:
Email
SMS
Guests typically received their highlight reel while still inside the venue, preserving the emotional momentum that drives sharing and repeat engagement.

Product Impact

  • The project transformed video delivery from an operational burden into a scalable revenue capability.
  • Venue staff no longer act as editors.
  • Guests leave with content worth sharing.
  • Management gains visibility across every session and every location.
  • Most importantly, the memory now travels beyond the venue itself.
  • Every delivered video becomes a marketing asset, a retention mechanism, and a new revenue opportunity generated automatically.
