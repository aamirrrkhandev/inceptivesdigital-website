The Business Challenge

  • Marketplaces fail for predictable reasons.
  • Customers do not trust providers.
  • Providers do not trust the platform.
  • Halwar Connect was facing both problems simultaneously.
  • Customers lacked visibility into pricing, service progress, and provider communication.
  • Providers lacked the operational tools required to manage bookings, earnings, schedules, and client relationships professionally.
  • The result was friction on both sides of every transaction.
  • And friction is expensive in marketplaces.
  • Because a marketplace only works when both sides decide to come back.

What We Built

  • Rather than building another booking application, we built an operating system for service transactions.
  • The platform consisted of three connected products designed around three completely different user groups.

Customer Application

  • Built with Flutter for iOS and Android.
  • Capabilities included:
  • Search local providers
  • Compare services
  • View transparent pricing
  • Book appointments
  • Track service progress
  • Communicate directly with providers
  • Complete payments securely
  • The objective was simple:
  • Remove uncertainty before money changes hands.

Provider Application

  • Also built with Flutter.
  • Providers gained a complete business management environment for:
  • Job acceptance
  • Availability management
  • Schedule control
  • Earnings tracking
  • Customer communication
  • Service delivery workflows
  • The platform became more than a lead source.
  • It became part of their daily operations.

Product
Strategy Decisions

Three architectural decisions shaped the platform.

Real-Time Communication

Communication between customers and providers remained inside the platform.

Users could:
  • Confirm service details
  • Send messages
  • Make voice calls
  • Share updates
Every interaction remained traceable and auditable.
The platform became the place where transactions happened rather than merely where they started.

Dynamic Pricing Transparency

Pricing disputes destroy marketplace trust.

The pricing engine was designed to surface changes instantly.

Customers could see:

  • Service costs
  • Add-on pricing
  • Provider adjustments
  • Share updates
Before confirming a booking.
Surprises disappeared from the transaction process.

Marketplace-Native Payments

Stripe Connect was selected to support marketplace-specific payment flows.

The platform supported:

  • Customer payments
  • Provider wallets
  • Direct payouts
  • Transaction History
  • Earnings visibility

Providers could trust the platform with their income because they could see exactly how and when they would be paid.
