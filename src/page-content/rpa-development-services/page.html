FAQ'S

Questions About Business Process Automation Services

What is RPA development?
RPA development is the creation of software bots that execute structured workflows across systems without manual input.
What processes can RPA automate?
Rule-based workflows such as data entry, reporting, reconciliation, onboarding, and system updates.
How is RPA different from AI automation?
RPA executes structured workflows. AI automation handles decision-making and unstructured data.
How long does RPA development take?
Most workflow automation systems take 3–8 weeks, depending on process complexity and system connections.
Can RPA connect to existing systems?
Yes. RPA connects to tools through APIs, connectors, and system-level integrations without replacing existing platforms.
What are the limitations of RPA?
RPA is designed for structured, rule-based workflows. It requires a clearly defined process logic to operate reliably.
Does RPA require maintenance?
Yes. Automation systems need updates as workflows and connected systems evolve.
How do you decide which process to automate?
Processes are evaluated based on structure, repetition, rule clarity, and operational volume during discovery.
